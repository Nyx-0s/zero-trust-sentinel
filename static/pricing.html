Questions

Pricing FAQ

What does "free risk score" actually include?

Everything, the first time: full external assessment of one domain — score, severity-ranked findings, business-impact summary, shareable report — in about two minutes. No card, no trial clock. One full free scan per domain; the free tier downgrades after that.

How does the 72-hour trial work?

Subscribe through PayPal and the first 72 hours are free; monitoring is active immediately. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you are not charged.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. Everything is generated from public data — no agents, no credentials, no traffic beyond what any visitor could send. That is also what makes the report safe to share externally.

What counts as a "domain"?

Your apex and everything discoverable beneath it: subdomains, live hosts, certificates. One subscription covers one organization's public surface.

How is chaos different from monitoring?

Monitoring watches your surface continuously from the public side and never touches anything. A chaos run actively attacks your assumptions — but only inside authorized targets, an agreed blast radius, and an emergency stop you control.

Do chaos runs need access to our systems?

Only the scope you authorize: explicit targets, blast radius, emergency stop. Nothing runs before those three exist, and a run that cannot confirm its scope stops and asks.

Why does the second score cost $1.99?

Because a score you never look at again is a screenshot, not a posture. The first scan proves the product; the $1.99 score keeps the number honest as your surface changes. On monitoring, every score after your first is free — that is what subscribing buys.

Do you change my score to scare me?

No. Every score is computed from your real public surface — nothing is ever inflated. What downgrades after the first scan is access, not truth: you keep seeing the real number, and we charge for the rest, because a stale score is the most dangerous number in security.

What is the Digital Twin?

A breach simulation, not another scan. It takes your real findings and runs 50 attack rounds against them, then shows the trajectory: how long an intrusion would likely live unseen, the impact window, the modeled revenue exposure, and how every number moves when you patch criticals, add edge controls, or turn on 24/7 response. Monitoring tier only — it is the difference between a check-up and a prognosis.

Are the simulation numbers predictions?

No — they are labeled models, and we say so on every trajectory. The inputs are real (your findings, severities, live hosts); the outputs are projections computed from assumptions that ship inside the report, including the revenue figure — set your own number and the exposure scales with it. We will never dress a model up as a measurement. It is there to make the conversation with your board concrete, not to frighten anyone with fiction.

Advisory · patched 21 October 2026

PHP2Shell

Two critical vulnerabilities in the PHP interpreter that can lead to unauthenticated remote code execution. PHP describes them as out-of-memory/request-state corruption and stale request state / use-after-free.

The facts

What is known, as published

CVEsCVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619
Disclosed21 October 2026 (PHP release notice, NCSC advisory)
Fixed versions8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35 (source backports also supplied for EOL 7.4, 8.0, 8.1)
In scopeAll PHP installations serving web requests: PHP-FPM, Apache mod_php, php-cgi, FrankenPHP, LiteSpeed. PHP notes mod_php as especially exposed.
Exploitation statusMixed reporting, by source and timestamp: Sophos cites an attempt reported by Amazon Threat Intelligence on 17 October and later attempts; CISA's 21 October alert stated it had not observed exploitation at publication; Huntress likewise reported no observation on its systems at its stated update times.
Official guidanceCISA and NCSC urged immediate inventory and patching, with systems that could not be patched promptly taken offline where feasible.
Read the exploitation row carefully. "Attempts reported by some vendors" and "no observation as of publication by CISA" are both accurate statements from different telemetry at different times. We publish both because a board briefing that picks only one is not a briefing — it is a pitch.
Your exposure

How to answer "are we patched?" today

Inventory what is public

You cannot patch what you have not found. Internet-facing PHP hides in admin panels, microsites, and legacy apps on forgotten subdomains.

Confirm the runtime version

Check each instance against the fixed releases above — including PHP behind a CDN or reverse proxy, where the version is not visible from the outside.

Prove it, date it

Record the version, the patch date and the asset owner. That record is what converts a panic into a process.

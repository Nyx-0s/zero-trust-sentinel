Context

Why this window feels different

19 October 2026

AI-agent intrusions reported

Reuters published an investigation into one attacker using AI agents to breach scores of sites via exposed instances, naming John Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins. Company statements qualified the impact — but the cost of running an attack has collapsed.

21 October 2026

PHP fixes released; CISA urges immediate patching

PHP shipped fixes for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. CISA's alert told organizations to inventory PHP and patch; it stated no exploitation had been observed as of its publication, while other vendors reported attempts. Both things are true — and both belong in your briefing.

21 October 2026

eMudhra certificate authority compromise detailed

Revocations and a Go trust denylist (CVE-2026-111943) followed the discovery that eight intermediate CA keys were recoverable. Cisco Talos reported no in-the-wild misuse as of 14:00 UTC that day.

22 October 2026

"Black October" enters the vocabulary

A widely shared post named the preceding 48 hours. Security coverage was quick to note that many of the underlying intrusions had been running for months before anyone said the words aloud.

What we will not do: claim your company is under attack, that these issues are being mass-exploited at scale, or that any specific customer is affected — without evidence specific to that claim. Exaggerated fear is easy to disprove, and disproving it costs the trust we need to protect you. We would rather hand you a date-stamped fact and a working fix.

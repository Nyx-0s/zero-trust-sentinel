Platform Overview

One pipeline: map, score, defend

Sentinel runs the same playbook a senior consultant would — enumeration, probing, validation, interpretation, remediation — as an autonomous swarm that finishes in minutes and repeats continuously.

Run it on your domain How scoring works
Stage 1

Discovery: what actually exists

You cannot defend an asset you never listed. Sentinel starts where an attacker starts.

🌐

Subdomain enumeration

Passive and active sources are merged into a full map of your apex — including the staging boxes and forgotten microsites nobody remembered.

🔌

Live host probing

Every candidate is probed for a real response, so the report reflects reachable services, not DNS noise.

🔒

TLS & chain analysis

Certificate validity, issuer chains and weak parameters are checked on every host — the check that matters most during a CA incident.

Stage 2

Analysis: findings become decisions

Raw output from scanners is worthless to a board. Our four-role analysis graph turns it into judgment.

Analyst

Reads the raw scan, discards false positives, and ranks what is genuinely exposed.

Fixer

Translates each confirmed finding into a concrete, copy-paste remediation.

Editor

Rewrites everything into plain business language with money-focused impact.

Critic

Challenges the draft: unsupported claims are cut. Evidence beats assertions.

The Critic is the point. Any vendor can generate a long list of CVEs. The discipline of removing what you cannot prove is what makes a report safe to put in front of an auditor.
Stage 3

Defense: posture that stays true

Continuous re-scanning

Your surface changes daily — new DNS records, new certificates, new subdomains. Sentinel re-scans so the score you publish remains defensible.

Prioritized remediation

Fixes arrive ordered by business impact, not by CVSS theater. See Remediation Plans.

Evidence on demand

Every assessment produces a white-label PDF you can hand to a customer, auditor or board. See Board Reporting.

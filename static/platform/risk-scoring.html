Risk Scoring

A single number you can defend

The Sentinel score runs 0–100. Higher is stronger. It is computed from what is publicly reachable right now — not from self-reported questionnaires or last year's penetration test.

The bands

What your score means

BandReadingWhat it triggers
80–100 Healthy posture — no material exposures found on the public surface. Minor findings listed; continuous monitoring recommended to hold the line.
60–79 Elevated — exposures that an attacker could chain. Remediation plan issued; re-scan scheduled; leadership informed.
0–59 Material exposure — findings with a direct path to breach. Immediate fix list; executive summary generated; board-ready PDF.
80 is the line. Below it, your perimeter shows material exposures during an active advisory window — which is exactly why the assessment displays a readiness warning alongside the score.
Inputs

What moves the number

Reachability

How much of your infrastructure answers from the public internet — subdomains, exposed services, forgotten environments.

Vulnerability posture

Known weaknesses detected on live hosts, weighted by exploitability and whether they are internet-facing.

TLS & certificate health

Expiry, weak parameters, and issuer chains — including chains that touch a certificate authority under active compromise.

Configuration hygiene

Header discipline, information leakage, admin surfaces left in public view, and DNS correctness.

Methodology

Why we publish the bands

A score that cannot be explained is a marketing prop. Every band above is fixed, visible on every report, and applied identically to every customer. The findings behind your number are always one click away, each with the observation that produced it.

That is also why our pipeline ends with a Critic pass: a claim that cannot be traced to something we observed on your public surface is removed before you ever see it.

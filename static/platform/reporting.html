Board Reporting

Documentation you can hand upward

When the board asks "are we secure," the answer that survives is the one with evidence attached. Sentinel produces a white-label PDF assessment built for exactly that conversation.

Inside the report

Structured for the reader who has five minutes

Executive summary

Plain-English posture in one page: the score, the material findings, the business impact, and what is being done. No jargon required to follow it.

Findings with evidence

Each finding carries the observation that produced it — the host, the header, the certificate, the response. Nothing is asserted without a source.

Remediation appendix

The prioritized fix list with owners and verification steps, ready to drop into your ticket system as work items.

Your brand, our rigor

White-label output: your logo and your colors for customer-facing or investor-facing versions. The methodology section stays intact either way.

Why it holds up

Evidence beats claims — in every document we produce

Reports are generated from public data only: no exploitation, no intrusive testing, no login attempts. That makes them safe to share outside your firewall, with customers who ask for a security review and with auditors who ask how you know.

The pipeline's final Critic pass removes anything we cannot trace to an observation on your surface. What reaches the PDF is defensible by construction.

During an advisory window the report also records the date range of the assessment — so "we were checked on 23 October, during the PHP2Shell disclosures" is a dated, provable statement rather than a recollection.
